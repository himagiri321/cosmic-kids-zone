ℹ️ About

Cosmic Kids Zone (కాస్మిక్ కిడ్స్ జోన్) is a free bilingual English + Telugu learning game for kids aged 1 to 10. Kids practise alphabets and spelling, maths, science and memory across 20 levels, and collect rockets, planets and stars as rewards.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Cosmic Kids Zone is made for children and respects their privacy.

Information we collect

None. There are no accounts, and we do not ask for names, emails, photos, location or contacts.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress and collected rockets, planets and stars are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Voice

The game reads words aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech. It does not use the microphone and records no audio.

Third parties

No ads, no analytics and no trackers. The game does not link to other websites.

Children

The game is suitable for ages 1 to 10. Parent or guardian supervision is recommended.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
